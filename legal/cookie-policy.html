1. Introduction to Cookies

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how SnapToolbox ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by an individual, uses them, and how you can manage them. By using the SnapToolbox website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used in web development to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and gather analytics data.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that:

  • Are stored on your device's browser
  • Contain information about your browsing activity and preferences
  • Can be read by the website that created them
  • Have an expiration date (session cookies expire when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain until their expiration date)
  • Help websites recognize returning visitors and customize their experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

SnapToolbox uses the following categories of cookies:

3.1 Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. These cookies are not contingent on consent and are used to:

  • Store your cookie consent preferences
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraud
  • Enable basic website functionality
  • Remember your session state

3.2 Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies collect information about how you use our website. They help us understand visitor behavior, website performance, and user engagement. These cookies are optional and require your explicit consent.

We use Google Analytics to collect and analyze website usage data. Google Analytics cookies track:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and device information
  • Operating system and screen resolution
  • Geographic location (country/region level)
  • Traffic sources and referral paths
  • User interactions and behavior patterns

Analytics cookies are loaded only if you consent through our cookie banner.

3.3 Advertising Cookies

Advertising cookies are used to display relevant advertisements and measure advertising effectiveness. These cookies are optional and require your explicit consent.

We use Google AdSense to manage advertisements on our website. AdSense cookies are used to:

  • Serve targeted advertisements based on your interests and browsing history
  • Measure ad performance and click-through rates
  • Prevent ad fraud and duplicate ad serving
  • Create user interest profiles for ad targeting

Advertising cookies are loaded only if you consent through our cookie banner.

7. Consent and Cookie Preferences

SnapToolbox is committed to GDPR and CCPA compliance. We obtain your consent for optional cookies through our cookie consent banner.

7.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit SnapToolbox, a cookie consent banner appears at the bottom of the page. You can:

  • Accept All: Consent to essential, analytics, and advertising cookies
  • Decline: Consent only to essential cookies necessary for website functionality
  • Manage Preferences: Customize which cookie types you consent to

7.2 Updating Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

  • Clicking the cookie consent button on SnapToolbox
  • Clearing your browser cookies and visiting SnapToolbox again
  • Manually adjusting your browser cookie settings

8. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most web browsers provide controls to manage cookies. You can typically:

  • View cookies: See what cookies are stored on your device
  • Accept/Reject cookies: Choose whether to allow specific cookies
  • Delete cookies: Remove all or specific cookies from your device
  • Block third-party cookies: Prevent third-party sites from setting cookies

8.1 Browser-Specific Instructions

Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox: Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data

Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and other site data

Internet Explorer: Tools > Internet Options > General > Browsing history > Delete

8.2 Do Not Track

Many browsers support a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. While SnapToolbox respects DNT signals where technically possible, not all third-party services (like Google Analytics and AdSense) honor DNT requests.

9. Cookie Preferences and Consent Management

SnapToolbox uses a consent management system to track your preferences. Your consent choice is stored in the snaptools_cookie_consent cookie with the following information:

  • Consent timestamp
  • Essential cookies: always true (required for functionality)
  • Analytics cookies: true/false based on your choice
  • Advertising cookies: true/false based on your choice

We do not load Google Analytics or Google AdSense scripts until you have explicitly consented.

10. Security and Data Protection

We implement security measures to protect cookie data from unauthorized access. However:

  • Cookies are stored on your device and are accessible to malware or unauthorized access to your device
  • We recommend keeping your browser and device software up to date
  • Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication on your accounts
  • Avoid using shared computers for sensitive activities

11. Retention and Deletion of Cookies

Cookie retention periods vary by type:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Retained until their expiration date or until you manually delete them
  • Consent Cookies: Retained indefinitely until you update preferences or clear browser data

You can delete all cookies by clearing your browser's browsing data/history.
