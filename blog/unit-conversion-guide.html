Unit Conversion Guide: Essential Conversions for Everyday Use

単位変換の豆知識 — Whether you're cooking a recipe from another country, buying clothing online from an international store, or working with engineering specs, unit conversion is an unavoidable daily challenge. This guide covers the most practical conversions with easy-to-remember approximations.

Why Unit Conversion Matters

The world uses two primary measurement systems: the metric system (SI units, used by most countries) and the imperial system (used primarily in the United States). Misunderstanding units has caused real-world problems ranging from failed diet goals to famous engineering disasters — the Mars Climate Orbiter was lost in 1999 partly due to a metric-imperial unit mismatch.

For everyday use, having a few key conversions memorized (or instantly available) prevents confusion and mistakes.

Length Conversions

1 inch = 2.54 cm

Used for screen sizes, clothing measurements, and US blueprints.

1 foot = 30.48 cm

Heights in the US are given in feet and inches.

1 mile = 1.609 km

US road distances and speed limits use miles.

1 yard = 0.914 m

Used in American football and fabric measurement.

Quick mental math: To convert miles to kilometers, multiply by 1.6. To convert km to miles, multiply by 0.6. For a rough check: 5 miles ≈ 8 km.

Weight and Mass Conversions

1 pound (lb) = 453.6 g

US body weight and grocery products use pounds.

1 ounce (oz) = 28.35 g

Common in US recipes and product packaging.

1 stone = 6.35 kg

Used in UK for body weight (14 pounds = 1 stone).

1 kg = 2.205 lbs

The most frequently needed kg-to-pounds conversion.

Quick mental math: To convert kg to lbs, multiply by 2.2. To convert lbs to kg, divide by 2.2 (or multiply by 0.45).

Temperature Conversions

Temperature conversion is one of the trickiest because it involves both multiplication and addition/subtraction — not just a simple ratio:

  • °C to °F: Multiply by 9/5 (or 1.8), then add 32. Example: 20°C = (20 × 1.8) + 32 = 68°F
  • °F to °C: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9 (or 0.556). Example: 98.6°F = (98.6 − 32) × 0.556 = 37°C

Key reference points: 0°C = 32°F (freezing) | 20°C = 68°F (room temp) | 37°C = 98.6°F (body temp) | 100°C = 212°F (boiling)

Area Conversions

  • 1 square meter (m²) = 10.76 square feet (ft²)
  • 1 acre = 4,047 m² ≈ 0.405 hectares
  • 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.47 acres
  • 1 square kilometer = 100 hectares = 247 acres

Area is often the most confusing category because conversion factors are squared. 1 foot = 0.305 m, but 1 square foot = 0.093 m² (0.305²).

Volume Conversions

  • 1 US gallon = 3.785 liters
  • 1 US cup = 236.6 mL
  • 1 tablespoon = 14.79 mL
  • 1 teaspoon = 4.93 mL
  • 1 fluid ounce = 29.57 mL

Note that US and UK (imperial) measurements differ for fluid ounces and gallons, which often causes confusion in recipes. A US gallon is about 3.79 L, while a UK gallon is 4.55 L.

Digital Storage Units

In the digital world, unit confusion is common between storage manufacturers and operating systems:

  • 1 KB (kilobyte) = 1,000 bytes (manufacturer definition) or 1,024 bytes (KiB, OS definition)
  • 1 MB = 1,000 KB or 1,024 KiB
  • 1 GB = 1,000 MB or 1,024 MiB
  • 1 TB = 1,000 GB or 1,024 GiB

This is why a "1 TB" hard drive shows as ~931 GB in Windows or macOS — the OS uses binary (1,024-based) calculations.
